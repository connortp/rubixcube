How to play

It's a twisty cube with a letter on every sticker. Once upon a time (this morning), every row on every face spelled a word. Then it got scrambled.

  • 👆
    Drag a letter to twist that row or column, just like a real cube.
  • 🌀
    Drag empty space (or use two fingers) to spin the whole cube around and look at the other sides.
  • 📌
    The ringed centre letter on each face never moves and always points up. Read rows left to right from its point of view. The middle slices can't turn.
  • ⭐
    Every row that spells a word is a point. Green = the original word is back. Yellow = a different real word, still a point!
  • ⛳
    Par is how many twists it took to scramble. Max score is 6 faces × rows.
  • 📤
    Hit Lock in when you're happy, then Share your score with the group chat.
  • 🏳️
    Stuck? Give up keeps your score and spins the cube back so you can see every word you missed.

Easy is a 3×3 with 3-letter words, Medium a 5×5 with 5-letter words, Hard a 7×7 with 7-letter words. Same cubes for everyone, new ones every midnight.
